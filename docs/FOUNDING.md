# NavKit Founding Principles

## Mission

Build a professional estimation and navigation software platform that
enables rapid development, validation, and deployment of embedded
navigation systems.

## Philosophy

NavKit emphasizes reusable engineering infrastructure rather than
vehicle or hardware specific implementations.

Core principles:

- Correctness before optimization.
- Rigorous statistical validation.
- Generic abstractions over one-off implementations.
- Strong typing, compile-time safety, and determinism.
- Desktop-first development with embedded deployment.
- Long-term maintainability.
- Modern C++23.
- Platform agnosticism.

## Scope

NavKit is intended to grow into a platform supporting inertial
navigation, multi-sensor fusion, simulation, analysis, and advanced
estimation research while remaining independent of any particular
vehicle, customer, or employer.
