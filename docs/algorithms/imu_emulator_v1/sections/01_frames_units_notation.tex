\chapter{Frames, Units, and Notation}

\section{Frames}

The emulator uses the same first-pass frame convention as
\texttt{navigator\_ecef\_v1}:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Symbol & Frame & Meaning \\
\midrule
$i$ & inertial & non-rotating inertial reference frame \\
$e$ & ECEF/PCPF & Earth-fixed frame rotating with Earth \\
$b$ & body/IMU & body frame collocated with the IMU and navigation state \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The v1 IMU emulator produces body-frame increments. It does not apply a lever
arm correction because the IMU point and body/navigation state point are the
same point by scope.

\section{Trajectory Truth Contract}

The v1 trajectory provider emits timestamped ECEF truth states:
\begin{align}
t_k, \qquad
\mathbf{p}_{eb,k}^{e}, \qquad
\mathbf{v}_{eb,k}^{e}, \qquad
C_{e,k}^{b}.
\label{eq:imu_trajectory_truth_contract}
\end{align}
Here $C_{e,k}^{b}$ maps ECEF-resolved vectors into the body frame at sample
$k$. If an implementation stores the inverse attitude, it must invert it before
using the equations in this document. The trajectory provider should not emit
$\omegab_{ib}^{b}$, $\fb_{ib}^{b}$, or IMU increments as primary truth fields.
Those are emulator outputs derived from the ECEF truth states.

\section{Signals and Code-Facing Names}

Truth inputs:
\begin{align}
\omegab_{ib}^{b} &\in \R^3
&& \text{angular rate of body wrt inertial, resolved in body},\\
\fb_{ib}^{b} &\in \R^3
&& \text{specific force of body wrt inertial, resolved in body}.
\end{align}

Raw interval outputs:
\begin{align}
\Delta\tilde{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{ib,k}^{b}
&\leftrightarrow
\texttt{delta\_theta\_ib\_b\_rad},\\
\Delta\tilde{\mathbf{v}}_{ib,k}^{b}
&\leftrightarrow
\texttt{delta\_v\_ib\_b\_mps}.
\end{align}

The output sample timestamp is the interval end time $t_{k+1}$ unless a runtime
format explicitly states otherwise. The interval duration is
\begin{equation}
\Delta t_k=t_{k+1}-t_k.
\end{equation}

\section{Truth, Raw, and Calibrated Quantities}

Unadorned symbols are true physical inputs. A tilde denotes raw measured IMU
outputs. A hat denotes calibrated or estimated quantities used downstream:
\begin{align}
\omegab_{ib}^{b} &&& \text{true angular rate},&
\tilde{\omegab}_{ib}^{b} &&& \text{raw gyro rate},&
\hat{\omegab}_{ib}^{b} &&& \text{calibrated gyro rate},\\
\fb_{ib}^{b} &&& \text{true specific force},&
\tilde{\fb}_{ib}^{b} &&& \text{raw accelerometer specific force},&
\hat{\fb}_{ib}^{b} &&& \text{calibrated accelerometer specific force}.
\end{align}

The emulator produces raw increments. The Navigator may apply inverse
calibration and estimated-bias correction before coning/sculling and
mechanization.
